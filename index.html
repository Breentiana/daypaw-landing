How DayPaw works

Three quick steps for the daycare day

1

See the week

Check places left each day against your 40-dog cap and add sample bookings only when there is room.

2

Check before check-in

Spot current, nearly due and expired vaccinations in the Today view. Expired sample records cannot be checked in.
